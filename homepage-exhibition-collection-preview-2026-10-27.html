Images that taught people how to transform the world.

Scientific drawings, illustrated manuals, and educational films helped people learn how to act on the natural world. This project explores the images and their uses in socialist China.

Project note

A collection shaped by questions, not completeness.

The collection begins with objects researched for the exhibitions. Each record preserves publication information and a short interpretive note. As new exhibitions are developed, the collection will extend beyond pest control into other forms of technical instruction.

Preview note: this is a working interface built from existing research records, not a final rights-cleared public catalogue. Historical image URLs are currently temporary and need migration to permanent local assets.
